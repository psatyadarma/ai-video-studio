# Playbook — Rangkumin Dong

## Brand
- Name: Rangkumin Dong · Tagline: "Berita ribet? Rangkumin dong."
- Handles: IG @rangkumindong · TikTok @rangkumindong · YouTube "Rangkumin Dong!" (@RangkuminDong-d2m)
- Audience: Indonesians who want the gist of business/market/tech/politics news, fast.
- Language: Bahasa Indonesia, casual but accurate.

## Format
- 60–90 s, 1080×1920, 30 fps. Sticker Explainer style (mg-styles-15 #07, MIT): cut-out stickers with white outline, flat icons, exact charts, one moving camera between zones, counters, word-highlight subtitles.
- Colours: paper #F3EFE6 · ink #1B2430 · slate #3E5568 · accent #FF5A36 · highlight #FFD23F.
- Structure (from 23 Oct): hook in today's hook style (first frame must work as a cover; see writer → Opening rules) → fixed tagline "Rangkumin dong!" right after the hook (kept as is — user 22 Oct: change only when the data says so) → 4–8 beats → "what happens next" → → "what happens next" → vote CTA ("Tim A atau Tim B? Komen!") → end card with sources.
- New visual every 2–4 s. **Safe area (from 19 Oct):** the camera frames each zone at 0.88× zoom, so zones need ~120 px clear space around them. Keep headlines, numbers and labels inside x 60–920 and y 240–1480: below the top bar, clear of the right-hand buttons and the bottom caption, and inside the 3:4 profile-grid crop (y 240–1680). Subtitles sit at y 1290, 56 px, max 3 lines. Check with `engine/safezone.py`.
- Always show "Bukan ajakan jual/beli saham" on market/stock topics.

## Real people as cut-outs (from 21 Oct; sample: tests/hook-person)
- For stories with a clear face (a minister, CEO, official), the hook can show them as a large cut-out sticker: rising on a paper "ledge" that hides the waist-up crop, name tag ("Nama · jabatan"), 3-beat label-bar headline over the torso (black → yellow → white), objects flying in (smoke, flags, money, logos) and a callout arrow.
- **Photos only from licensed sources:** Wikimedia Commons (public domain / CC0 / CC BY / CC BY-SA; Indonesian government official portraits are usually public domain), official government pages, company press kits. Never news-agency or random web photos.
- Tools: `engine/commons.py search "<name>"` → `get "<File:…>" accounts/rangkumindong/assets/people <slug>` (saves `<slug>.json` with the credit), then `engine/cutout.py <slug>.jpg <slug>.png --kind person` (objects: `--kind object`). Reuse `assets/people/` before downloading again. Commons rate-limits: use standard thumbnail widths (960) and retry after a minute.
- Show the credit small on screen while the photo is visible and in the end card/caption ("Foto: …, via Wikimedia Commons").
- Rules: no fake quotes (attribute: "kata …"), no mocking or distorting edits, same treatment for all political sides, no private individuals.

## Script & delivery ("punchy", approved 19 Oct)
- Conversational, explaining to a friend. Short sentences. Openers like "Jadi gini.", "Pemicunya? X.", "Nah,", "Tapi tenang,".
- Dramatic beat on the key number ("Dalam. Satu. Hari.").
- Every number on screen is spoken; every claim tied to a source.
- Per-line ElevenLabs v3 tags (shocked / serious / skeptical / reassuring / playful). Numbers and acronyms spelled out in `say` ("em es si ai", "sebelas koma lima tujuh").
- **Acronyms (user feedback 21 Oct, "BNPB"):**
  - Spelled-out acronyms (BNPB, KPK, OJK, BMKG, BUMN, DPR…): write the letters in `say` as Indonesian letter names separated by spaces only, e.g. "Be En Pe Be", "Ka Pe Ka", "O Je Ka", "Be U Em En". **No full stops between letters** ("Be. En. Pe. Be." made ~200 ms gaps, which the user said is too slow). Fast-run spellings ("be en pe be" glued to the previous word) got heard as "BMKG".
  - Put a **comma before the acronym** so there is a short breath after the previous word: "Per enam belas Oktober, Be En Pe Be catat …". The user wants a clear ~0.2 s pause *before* the acronym and the letters themselves **quick and tight** (approved: whole acronym ≈0.9 s, gaps between letters ≈35–40 ms).
  - Acronyms that are normally said as words stay words: Bappenas, Basarnas, Kemenhut, Danantara, Polri ("pol-ri"), Satgas.
  - If a take still comes out wrong, fix it without a new take: insert ~200 ms room tone before the first letter and speed only the letter run 1.3–1.45× (pitch kept), then shift timings (see videos/2026-10-21-karhutla-lutfi-singapura/build/pre-gap). Always listen-check acronyms with a short speech-to-text clip before QA.
- Pauses capped at 0.24 s; target ~150–170 spoken wpm.
- Voice A/B: male Pratama vs female Cahaya, 7-day blocks. Taglines are fixed recordings in `assets/voice/`.

## Topic selection (daily)
- Maintain `events.csv` (source of truth; `events.md` is generated by `engine/events.py render`): every morning add new events from the last 24–48 h (date, category, key numbers, storyline, status, sources), resolve "Open checks", move rows older than 14 days to the archive.
- Generate ideas from the **whole 14-day window** (not just today): new developments, follow-ups on active storylines, upcoming dated events (e.g. BI RDG), and explainers that connect several events.
- Send the user **exactly 5** ideas, ranked, with one marked recommended, then the 3 **runner-ups** (outranked today, not rejected).
- **Virality rubric (from 21 Oct):** six signals, each 0–2, total /12: **spreading** (most-read lists / trending today), **wallet** (changes what people pay, earn or can do), **surprise** (shock number, reversal, conflict, common misconception), **visual** (lands in 3 s), **fresh** (today/yesterday or a date within 3 days), **rivals** (few explainers have covered it). Rank mainly by score; the day's topic only breaks near-ties. Virality never overrides fact-checking or the sensitivity rules. Every week the analyst compares predicted scores with real views, watch % and shares, and proposes re-weighting signals that don't predict.
- **Weekly topic rhythm (soft):** Mon markets, economy & business · Tue tech · Wed viral (general news people are talking about) · Thu politics (neutral, sourced) · Fri wildcard (highest score, any topic) · Sat weekly long video 08:00 + a Short cut from its strongest chapter · Sun "Minggu Depan" (what to watch this week: BI dates, data releases, court dates, deadlines).

## Daily news sweep checklist (so nothing big is missed)
Search each of these every morning (last 24–48 h), not just generic "berita hari ini":
- Institutions: Presiden, **Wapres**, DPR/MPR, **MK**, MA, KPK, Kejagung, KPU/Bawaslu, Polri/TNI, BI, OJK, BEI, BPS, Kemenkeu, key ministries, Danantara/BUMN, regional heads.
- Outlets' most-read / "terpopuler" lists: Kompas, Detik, CNN Indonesia, Tempo, Antara, CNBC Indonesia, Kontan, Bisnis.
- Fact-check feeds: Komdigi hoaks, Cek Fakta.
- Categories: markets, economy/policy, business, tech, politics & law, national news, environment/disasters, global-with-Indonesia-impact.

## Agent pipeline (from 20 Oct 2026)
Roles live in `.claude/agents/` (shared researcher rules: `.claude/shared/researcher-common.md`). The main chat is the coordinator and the user's only contact; agents never message the user. Hand-offs are files in `accounts/rangkumindong/daily/<date>/`, progress in its `status.json` (`engine/day.py`).
1. 06:00 WITA: `day.py init` → 4 researchers in parallel (news-markets, news-politics, news-business, news-national; Sonnet) → each writes `sweep-<area>.json`, validated by `day.py check`. Rerun only an area whose file fails validation (no second sweeps; gaps are noted instead).
2. `day.py merge` adds new events to `events.csv` (dedup) and renders `events.md`.
3. writer (mode ideas, **Sonnet**) → `ideas.md` (exactly 5 + 3 runner-ups) → **light** fact-check (hooks and why-now only) → coordinator sends ideas + any fact-check flags to the user.
4. User picks → writer (mode draft, Opus) → video folder with `script.json`, `draft.md`, `post.json`, `claims.json` → **full** fact-check → fixes applied → draft sent for approval.
5. After approval: production scripts (voice, render, mix) → **qa-reviewer** agent (subtitle sync via speech-to-text, visuals frame by frame, numbers vs approved draft, loudness, format, disclaimers) → fix and re-check until PASS → only then send the video to the user. Posting script only after "OK post".
6. Analyst: metrics at 24 h / 72 h, weekly review on Sunday.
Weekly long video ("Rangkumin Seminggu", Sat 08:00 WITA): writer + fact-checker Friday morning → after approval, chapter-builders in parallel (one per chapter) → assemble → editor reviews the whole cut → fixes → render → schedule.

## Learning the user's preferences
- `knowledge/preferences.md`: **"What the user told us"** (the coordinator appends a dated line whenever the user comments on topics, angles or formats) + **"What the picks show"** (recomputed by `engine/prefs.py` from offers vs picks after every pick: pick rank, recommended-or-not, storylines picked or skipped, sensitivity).
- The writer reads it before ranking. Preference is a tiebreaker (+1), never an override, and 1 of the 5 ideas always stays outside the usual pattern so tastes can evolve.
- Sunday review: the analyst compares preference fit with real performance. When the user's picks and the audience disagree, it says so.

## Covered topics (no accidental repeats)
- `knowledge/covered.csv` (via `engine/covered.py`) logs every idea **offered** and every video **drafted/posted**: date, title, angle, key numbers, storyline, PART, events, folder, status, links.
- The writer runs `covered.py check` on every candidate. A posted topic only comes back with a real new development, as a follow-up (PART n). Ideas offered before but not picked are labelled so.
- Coordinator updates status: offered → picked → drafted → posted (with links) or rejected.

## Continuity (videos build on each other)
- Before picking topics, check `storylines/` for active stories with new developments or milestones due.
- Follow-up videos: hook references the last episode ("Minggu lalu IHSG anjlok 11%. Sekarang…"), show a small "PART n" badge, reuse the same chart/visual language, and link the previous video in the pinned comment.
- Each video ends a storyline file update: new facts + sources, link to the video, next milestone.

## Fact-check & sensitive topics
- 2+ independent sources per claim; prefer originals (BPS, BI, OJK, IDX, ministries, company filings, MSCI etc.).
- Re-check fast-moving numbers right before rendering; log any change from the approved draft.
- Politics: neutral, what happened / what each side says / why it matters. No allegations without official sources, no opinions, no party colours.
- Never invent data points for charts; plot only verified values.

## Approval & posting
- **Two touches (from 23 Oct):** the user picks a topic and gives "OK post" on the finished video; no separate draft approval (HANDOFF → Standing rules, incl. the default pick at 09:00 / 14:00 WITA). The draft is still written, fact-checked and sent **together with the video**. It always contains: topic + angle, full script (subtitle text), visual plan, sources, and the **captions**: IG caption (with hashtags), YouTube title (+ #Shorts), YouTube description, tags, and the sources first comment.
- Captions: hook line + 3 bullet summary + vote question + "Bukan ajakan jual/beli saham" (market topics) + "Sumber di komentar" + 6–8 hashtags incl. #rangkumindong. Same numbers as the video.
- User gives "OK post" (with time slot) before anything is published.
- **Early slots:** if the post time is before 10:00 WITA (e.g. the weekly video, Sat 08:00), the finished video and captions go to the user the evening before, so "OK post" can be given before bed and the post is scheduled overnight.
- **TikTok captions: at most 5 hashtags** (TikTok's limit). Pick the 5 strongest, always including #rangkumindong. IG/YouTube keep 6–8.
- **TikTok, manual until it's connected (24 Oct 2026):** with every finished video, send the MP4 plus a ready-to-paste TikTok caption (IG text with ≤5 hashtags; TikTok links aren't clickable, so sources go in a comment the user posts and pins). The user posts it by hand.
- **Covers/thumbnails:** Shorts/Reels: the first frame is designed to work as the cover (hook text big and readable); when the IG cover can be set via Upload-Post, use a still of the hook frame. Weekly long video: a custom 1280×720 thumbnail (big number or face-free sticker composition + 3–4 word title) is part of its draft.
- Posting via Upload-Post (engine/post.py, profile "Rangkumindong"): IG Reels + YouTube Shorts (#Shorts in title). TikTok: user warms the account up manually until 24 Oct 2026, then connect it to Upload-Post (AI-generated label on). Pin a sources comment.
## Experiments (parallel A/B, started 20 Oct 2026)
Goal: learn fast without waiting weeks per variable. Every video carries experiment tags in `video-log.md`.
0. **Current design (from 23 Oct, user OK 22 Oct): daily opening A/B.** Every day the main video (A, today's `hook`) is posted as usual, and a B version that differs **only in the opening** (today's `hook_b`, from `script.json → hook_b`) is made with `engine/variant.py` (one short voice call, body spliced from A's take) and posted to **Instagram only as a Trial Reel** (`engine/post.py --trial`, non-followers). Hook styles: question_reveal · shock_number · kamu_stakes · myth · person_collage · story (brand.json → experiments.hook_styles); every pair meets twice with sides swapped through 21 Nov. **Subtitles alternate daily** (`subs`: word = 1–2 words big and centred, after @mindwander_dot_id; block = the current 3-line highlight), the same for A and B that day; the producer sets `window.SUB_MODE`. Compare A vs B on IG 3-s hold first, then avg % watched. **Voice is frozen in weekly blocks and the slot at 19:00 WITA** until the hook test is decided; format alternates standard / short on weekdays (person_hook is now a hook style, not a format). Older rows below are history.
1. **Rotating design (20–22 Oct, superseded).** Each day's video gets one value of each variable, following `brand.json → experiments.schedule` (balanced so every combination appears): voice (Pratama/Cahaya) × post slot (12:30 / 19:00 WITA) × hook type (question / shock number). After ~12 videos every value has ~6 samples; compare averages.
1b. **Format test (from 23 Oct).** Weekdays rotate `format` (brand.json → experiments.schedule): **standard** (current 60–90 s explainer) · **person_hook** (same body, but a real-person cut-out hook in the first 2 s in the **collage look** — copy `tests/hook-collage/index.html` (style after mg-styles-15 #06, MIT), then cut to the sticker body; the collage render is slow (~1 s/frame), so keep it to the hook; falls back to standard if no licensed face fits, and is logged as standard) · **short** (30–45 s, ~80–110 spoken words: hook, tagline, 2–3 beats, CTA). Sat (chapter cut) and Sun (Minggu Depan) are outside the test. Reason: IG avg % watched was 12.4% (video #1) and 3.1% (video #2), so the drop-off is in the first seconds. Every video's `script.json` carries `"format"`; the dashboard and the Sunday review compare avg % watched by format. Same "decided" rule as the other variables.
2. **Instagram Trial Reels (same-day head-to-head).** For high-value topics, render a B version (other voice or other hook — only the variable under test changes) and post it as a Trial Reel, shown to non-followers first. Compare A vs B on the same topic and day. Costs ~2k ElevenLabs characters + ~7 min render.
3. **Not used for testing:** comparing platforms against each other (IG vs YouTube audiences differ) — platforms are reported separately.
4. **Metrics** (at 24 h and 72 h, per platform, from Upload-Post's post analytics): views, avg % watched, shares, saves, follows gained. Primary = avg % watched, measured on YouTube (average view percentage) and TikTok (average time watched ÷ length); Instagram: Upload-Post's post analytics does return `ig_reels_avg_watch_time` (ms) for Reels (found 20 Oct), so avg % watched = that ÷ video length; also compare reach and shares + saves per 1k views. YouTube analytics lag: 0 views/0% at 24 h with comments present = not reported yet → log n/a, not 0. Tie-breaker everywhere = shares + saves per 1k views.
5. **Decisions:** weekly review (Sunday). A variable is "decided" when one value wins on avg % watched by ≥15% relative over ≥5 videos each and isn't explained by one viral outlier. Then lock it and start the next variable (length 60 vs 90 s, visual density, CTA style, series vs standalone).
