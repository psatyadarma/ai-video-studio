## MODE ideas
Inputs: all `sweep-*.json` in the day folder + `python3 -I engine/events.py window accounts/rangkumindong/knowledge 14`.
Write `ideas.md` with **exactly 5** ideas, ranked, one marked ⭐ recommended.
**Scoring:** give every candidate the virality rubric score (playbook → Topic selection): six signals 0–2 (spreading, wallet, surprise, visual, fresh, rivals), total /12. Start from the researchers' `signals`, then re-check `spreading` yourself just before writing: WebFetch the latest headlines (cnbcindonesia.com, detik.com, cnnindonesia.com, antaranews.com) because stories move between the sweep and now. Score the strongest angle of a story (a conflict or a human story counts, not only a new number). Rank mainly by score; the day's topic only breaks near-ties (within 1 point); storyline follow-ups get +1 when the earlier part did well. Consider the whole 14-day window: new developments, storyline follow-ups ("PART n"), upcoming dated events, explainers connecting several events; weigh today's topic rotation but don't force it. **No accidental repeats:** for every candidate run `python3 -I engine/covered.py check accounts/rangkumindong/knowledge "<title + angle + key numbers>"`. If a posted video comes up, only keep the idea when there is a real new development, then frame it as a follow-up (PART n, hook referencing the earlier video). If it was offered before but not picked, say so ("ditawarkan 19 Okt") so the user knows.
Per idea (phone-readable, short):
- **Hook** in Bahasa Indonesia, written in today's hook type (question or shock number)
- Why now (1 line) · key numbers · storyline (new / continues X, PART n) · sensitivity
- 2+ source links (from the sweep/events, never invented)
- Covered check: `NEW`, or what it repeats/continues (date + title)
- **Must-offer rule (22 Oct lesson):** any story at #1–#3 of an outlet's most-read list in the last 24 h must appear as one of the 5 ideas or a runner-up, scored on its best angle. "No decision yet" or "no new number" is never a reason to drop it. (On 21 Oct we skipped the Greenpeace climb at Bundaran HI — detik terpopuler #1 — for that reason; a brand-new account, @dijelasin_ya, posted "Naik monumen 30 meter?" the same day and got 57K likes on its very first post.)
- **Angle types — pick the one people are already asking:** (a) **how it happened** — a step-by-step reconstruction ("Langkah 1: pasang tali … Langkah 4: turun pelan") with a date · place header card and an illustrated scene of the real location; neutral by design, because it explains the mechanics, not who's right; (b) **scale** (below); (c) **what it means for you**; (d) **myth vs fact**; (e) **who's who**. Viral spectacles (stunts, accidents, odd sights) usually want (a).
- **Scale angle (from 22 Oct, learned from Semenit Ilmu):** whenever a story has a size, amount or count, also offer a "segede apa?" comparison angle with a held-back answer — e.g. burned area as "berapa kali Jakarta?", a budget as "per orang dapat berapa?", debt as "kalau ditumpuk uang Rp100 ribu, setinggi apa?". The title is a question whose answer is a number revealed near the end; the visual (map overlay, stack, scale bar) *is* the answer. Use only verified numbers and say the method in the caption (e.g. "luas lahan terbakar KLHK, dibandingkan luas DKI 661 km²").
- **Weights:** use `brand.json → rubric_weights` (default 1.0 each) for the total: weighted sum scaled back to /12, rounded. Show the raw signals as usual.
- **Score line:** `🔥 9/12 · spreading 2 · wallet 1 · surprise 2 · visual 2 · fresh 2 · rivals 0` plus the spreading evidence (e.g. "CNBC terbaru, 20 Okt")
After the 5, add **"Runner-ups"**: the next 3 candidates with their score line and one sentence on what they'd be good for (e.g. "strong for Thursday", "good PART 2 if it develops"). Runner-ups are not rejected, only outranked today.
Top line of the file: today's experiment arm (voice · slot · hook · **format**). If today's hook or hook_b is person_collage, prefer ideas with a clear face and say which licensed photo you'd use; if it's short, prefer ideas that work in 30–45 s.

## MODE draft
Input: the chosen idea number (and any notes from the user). Create the video folder `accounts/rangkumindong/videos/<RUN_DATE>-<slug>/` and write:
**Length by voice (lessons 20–21 Oct):** Cahaya reads ~130 wpm → ≤190 spoken words; Pratama ~150 wpm → ≤215. Keep `direction` under 60 characters. Lines over ~20 words get split into subtitle pages. Plan a visual beat every 2–3 s on long lines.

**Opening rules (retention, from 23 Oct — IG viewers left after 2.7–8.8 s):**
- The first spoken line states the stakes or the question in ≤8 words, starting at 0.0 s — not a bare number without context ("Rp18.046 per dolar." ✗ → "Rupiah tembus 18 ribu. Gaji kamu kena?" ✓). Today's hook type decides the flavour, the rule applies to all.
- Frame 1 carries the whole premise as a big 2-line title card (≤6 words, e.g. "JAWA PINDAH KE EROPA"), readable as the cover; it stays on screen for the first ~3 s.
- Something moves or changes every 1–1.5 s in the first 5 s (pop, zoom, number counting, map move). No logo intro, no "Halo", no "Jadi gini" restart after the hook.
- Open a loop the video pays off near the end ("angka terakhirnya yang paling bikin kaget") and pay it off.
- **A turn at 60–70 % of the runtime** (after @mindwander_dot_id): a "Tapi…" moment that changes how the viewer sees the story (the part most people miss, the second-order effect, what it means for them).
- **End on a question people can answer in their own words** (what would you do / which side do you think is right / what did we miss?), not only a Tim A / Tim B vote. Stay neutral: the question invites opinions, the video doesn't give one.
- **Story hook style:** open on one ordinary person in a scene, mid-action ("Jumat pagi, layar Pak Budi merah semua."), no context first; the news arrives through what happens to them. Fictional everyman examples are fine if labelled as an illustration ("misalnya") and every number is real.
